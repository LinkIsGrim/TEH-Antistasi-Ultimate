# War Tier Zero — Quest Guide

War Tier Zero is a pre-Antistasi start. You do not begin as a registered rebel. You are just a civilian in an occupied town, with very little money, no rebel support, no rebel radio, and no official access to the usual Antistasi services.

HQ location is assigned at random and remains hidden while you are proving yourself. Do not worry, Petros will behave until you get there.

## Your goal

Your first objective is to become accepted by the resistance.

To do that, you need to:

* Find a proper primary weapon. A shotgun does not count.
* Find a vest-slot item, such as a vest, chest rig, or belt. It does not have to be armored, but armor helps.
* Prove yourself enough to have a positive score. Shooting enemies increases score; dying reduces it.
* Find a resistance contact and join properly.

Until then, HQ does not know who you are. Fast travel, arsenal access, garage access, support calls, squad commands, and money transfers may be unavailable.

## How to start

Talk to civilians, marked in purple on the map.

Some people know nothing. Some are afraid. Some may be sympathetic. Asking the wrong person the wrong question can attract police attention, so do not treat every civilian like a quest marker with legs.

You are undercover at the start, but undercover does not mean invincible. Walking around like a normal civilian is safer than waving weapons around in the street. Dogs can still bark you out, and hijacking a car will increase your wanted level.

## Finding weapons

You are expected to obtain your first gear locally. Stealing a car to go somewhere else may be an option if you can handle it.

Possible early routes include:

* Asking civilians about firearms and following rumors about hidden weapons, including a repeatable “Bob’s quest” that can lead to a crate of rifles. Be careful and check loyalty before asking dangerous questions.
* Buying a basic pistol from a resistance contact.
* Salvaging weapons from criminals or patrols. You can use a car, or wait for an accident to happen.
* Visiting the nearest Military Administration, which will happily provide you with some weapons or bullets, usually in your general direction.

A pistol may help you get started, but joining the resistance requires a primary weapon, so choose carefully where you want to invest.

## Finding a contact

Locals may point you toward a resistance contact. Look for someone connected to the “green” side of things.

When you find a contact, they can tell you whether you are ready to join. Using that option also enables a named marker, so you can find them later if needed.

If they refuse you, you are probably missing one of the requirements: a proper primary weapon, a vest or holster, or enough proof that you are not just another informant.

## What happens if you die?

If you die before becoming a registered rebel, the resistance does not recover you. You start over as another unknown civilian in a random town. If that leads you back to your previous body, you are lucky.

Note that your score remains negative if you respawn too much.

Once you are accepted into the resistance, HQ and the Antistasi campaign await.

## Gearing tips

* Sometimes a civilian behind the wheel is more dangerous than a rebel.
* A shotgun is better than nothing, even if it does not qualify you for joining.
* Policemen of most factions wear vests or holsters. SWAT teams always wear vests and helmets.
* Bob’s gun crate always contains at least one rifle and at least three magazines, following loot settings.
* SWAT cars summoned during the quest contain one special rifle, accessories, and five magazines.
* Each SWAT car increases the chance of a QRF being called, so greed is fine, but if you die, you will have to start over.
* Military Administration removes undercover, so you have to plan your infiltration in advance. It is doable even with no weapons if you act quickly.

War Tier Zero may get you killed by police, patrols, SWAT, QRF, or whatever your bad decisions attracted. It rewards greed only if you survive it.
